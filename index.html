    Put this app on your iPhone home screen

    In Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up), then tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. The app then opens full screen from its own icon and works without internet after the first visit (audio needs internet).

    About the English spellings

    The English lyrics follow the book's spelling code: • Capital vowels are long: A = ā (rAma = Rāma), I = ī, U = ū, E = ē, O = ō • Capital T, Th, D, Dh, N = hard sounds ṭ, ṭh, ḍ, ḍh, ṇ (as in maNi) • L = ḷ (ळ), zh = ழ, S = ṣ (ष), sh = ś (श) • c = च (as in church), ch = छ, gn = ज्ञ • r between two consonants = ṛ (krSNa = Kṛṣṇa, krpA = Kṛpā) In the index, titles are shown with these marks (Shrī Rāma) so they read naturally. The lyrics are exactly as printed in the book.

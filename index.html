🪷Our Purpose

Harinamadwar was born from a simple vision – to spread the joy of Hari Nama Sankeertanam and the timeless teachings of our saints through music, bhajans and meaningful satsangs.

What began as a small satsang has blossomed into a movement of devotion, supported by countless devotees and families.

🎵Our Satsangs

  • Soulful bhajans, namavalis & kirtans
  • Live music with dedicated artists on veena, flute, harmonium, mridangam, dolki & morsing
  • Uplifting, immersive & participative – for all ages
  • A space to feel peace, devotion and togetherness

📖Meaning That Connects

We go beyond singing. Each song is explained with the deeper meaning, context & bhava – the message of the saints, stories behind the compositions and how they can inspire our daily lives.

This helps every listener – from children to senior citizens – connect, understand and experience the beauty of bhakti.

🙏Learn. Sing. Live Bhakti.

We conduct FREE learning sessions to teach bhajans and kirtans of various saints in a simple, structured and joyful way.

Let’s keep our rich devotional traditions alive in our homes and hearts.

🛕A Journey of Grace

With the Lord’s infinite grace and the love of our hosts, we undertook a sankalpam to conduct nama sankeertanam in 108 places. By that grace, this journey has now continued well beyond 156 gatherings.

Numbers are not our goal – spreading devotion and touching hearts is.

💜Benefits of Nama Sankeertanam

  • Calms the mind & reduces stress
  • Encourages positive emotions
  • Builds emotional resilience
  • Enhances focus & concentration
  • Fosters harmony, compassion & a sense of belonging
  • Creates a joyful & sattvic atmosphere at home and in society

🌼Guided by Devotion. Led in Service.

Harinamadwar is guided by Shri Janakiraman, originally from Srirangam and based in Bangalore. After a long journey in leadership and management, he has chosen to dedicate himself fully to the service of Hari Nama Sankeertanam.

With humility and devotion, he continues this mission with one prayer – may more homes, temples and communities become centers for nama bhakti and collective spiritual joy.

“Where there is Nama,
there is Peace.”

🏠Invite Harinamadwar

We warmly welcome families, temples, communities & organizations to host Hari Nama Sankeertanam satsangs filled with bhakti, music, meaning and divine grace.

    About the English spellings

    The English lyrics follow the book's spelling code: • Capital vowels are long: A = ā (rAma = Rāma), I = ī, U = ū, E = ē, O = ō • Capital T, Th, D, Dh, N = hard sounds ṭ, ṭh, ḍ, ḍh, ṇ (as in maNi) • L = ḷ (ळ), zh = ழ, S = ṣ (ष), sh = ś (श) • c = च (as in church), ch = छ, gn = ज्ञ • r between two consonants = ṛ (krSNa = Kṛṣṇa, krpA = Kṛpā) In the index, titles are shown with these marks (Shrī Rāma) so they read naturally. The lyrics are exactly as printed in the book.
